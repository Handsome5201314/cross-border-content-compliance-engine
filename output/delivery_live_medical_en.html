objections

  • topic

    price

    question

    What is the cost of the software subscription and the smart badge?

    answer

    The software operates on a subscription model with pricing tailored to hospital size and needs. The smart badge is currently on the roadmap, so its pricing is not yet determined. We can provide a detailed quote for the software upon request.

  • topic

    deployment

    question

    Does this require significant changes to our existing IT infrastructure?

    answer

    No, the system is designed for local deployment on provided hardware (RTX 3090 GPU). It operates independently of your existing IT infrastructure and does not require cloud connectivity, minimizing integration complexity.

  • topic

    security

    question

    How do you ensure patient data security and privacy?

    answer

    All data processing occurs locally on hospital servers. Raw audio and images are deleted immediately after processing, and no data is sent to external cloud APIs. The knowledge base is encrypted and backed up daily. This design ensures data remains within the hospital's control.

  • topic

    certification

    question

    Do you have FDA or CE certification for this product?

    answer

    We do not currently hold FDA or CE certifications. Compliance with local regulations such as GDPR or regional data laws must be verified by the hospital according to their specific market requirements. Our focus is on providing a tool for clinical documentation support, not diagnostic decision-making.

  • topic

    languages

    question

    Does the system support languages other than English?

    answer

    Yes, the system supports English, Arabic, Indonesian, Thai, Vietnamese, Malay, and Russian. This makes it suitable for hospitals in Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and other regions.

  • topic

    support

    question

    What kind of technical support do you offer?

    answer

    We provide technical support for software installation, configuration, and usage. This includes assistance with setting up the local environment and troubleshooting any issues that may arise during operation.
